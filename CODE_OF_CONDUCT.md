# Code of conduct

MeerkaT3 welcomes contributors and users of every background and experience level.
Everyone participating in this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project discussions is expected to follow this policy, including maintainers.

## Working together

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code, not the person proposing them.
- Explain disagreements with evidence, listen to feedback, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Help newcomers understand the project without dismissing their questions.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries. Do not post private conversations
  or personal information without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and retaliation against someone raising a concern are not
acceptable. Technical disagreement and good-faith criticism are welcome.

## Reporting a concern

For abusive content on GitHub, use **Report content** on the relevant issue,
pull request, or comment, or **Block or report** on a user's profile. Follow
[GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam),
which also link to the GitHub Support contact form. Reports submitted to GitHub
Support are handled by GitHub. If **Report to repository admins** is offered,
that option sends the report to this project's maintainers.

Include links and enough context to identify the behavior. Do not publish sensitive
details in a public issue. For a non-sensitive question about this policy or a
moderation decision, open an
[issue](https://github.com/lukacf/meerkat-t3/issues) for the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, remove inappropriate
content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation. Responses should
reflect the severity and pattern of the behavior; serious abuse can result in an
immediate restriction. Maintainers should explain a moderation decision when doing
so does not expose private information or put someone at risk.
